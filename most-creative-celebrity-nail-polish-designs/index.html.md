# 9 Most Creative Celebrity Nail Polish Designs to Try Right Now

Beauty trends are starting everywhere with these creative celebrity nail polish designs! Hollywood A-listers never hold back when it comes to making a fashion statement, and their adventurous choices offer fantastic inspiration for what is trending right now. From classic color palettes like smoky rose nude to striking pearly pink accents, exploring these red-carpet styles is a fun way to elevate your everyday manicure. Take a look at some of the most creative celebrity nail polish designs and find your next signature look!

## 1. Mod Manis

![Mod Manis](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/19092809_2790_550x550.jpg)

The retro-inspired tip design is back in a big way! Zooey Deschanel, the hipster queen, has often rocked this vintage look on the red carpet with classic elegance. Creating this style at home is surprisingly simple using a timeless black and white color combination. Using a thin striper brush gives you total control over detailed polka dots and line work. If you want a softer alternative to high-contrast geometric art, consider swapping for a [nude matte finish](#4) or adding subtle endless pearl highlights. I love rocking this look with a simple outfit because it provides a cool and funky pop! This is definitely one of the most creative celebrity nail polish designs.

Here are a few quick tools to help you get clean retro lines:

- Striper brush or precision dotting tool

- High-contrast base shade like black, white, or deep navy

- Quick-dry top coat for a smudge-free shine

## 2. Pearly Tips

![Pearly Tips](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/28122845_7157_600x402.jpg)

Another cool look that is trending is the half-moon mani. It’s a modern take on the classic French tip where the tip color takes up at least half of your nail, making the bottom half look like a crescent moon. Jennette McCurdy, star of Nickelodeon’s Sam & Cat, rocks this look all the time! To add a little more creativity, she added pearl accents to the moon half of the nail. Adding soft pearly pink hues or micro-pearl studs elevates the design into something classy and chic. You can easily test out premium polishes from established brands like [Essie](https://www.essie.com) for smooth coverage.

## 3. Neon Nails

![Neon Nails](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/19093524_0641_500x375.jpg)

Bright electric shades are a fantastic choice when you want your manicure to pop. The bright colors look gorgeous on tanned skin and look so flirty and fun! Pretty Little Liars star Shay Mitchell posts tons of manicures on her Instagram account! Combining a bunch of neon colors still manages to look like it belongs together. The designs are limitless, with bright pink and green being top favorites. Pairing vibrant colors with a [stylish ombre effect](#7) gives the look even more depth and dimension.

## 4. Nude Matte

![Nude Matte](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/28123052_6778_600x398.jpg)

The nude nail color is exceptionally chic and timeless. It shows how put together you are without looking like you are trying too hard, going great with any skin tone. The matte finish modernizes this classic look instantly. Chloe Grace Moretz showed off her nude nails at the MTV Movie Awards, demonstrating how a velvety top coat updates a shade like smoky rose nude. High-end lines from [OPI](https://www.opi.com) feature extensive nude color ranges suited for any style.

## 5. Glitter Glam

![Glitter Glam](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/28123309_2910_600x420.jpg)

One of my favorite things to do when painting nails is adding some glitter polish! If you ever smudge a nail, the glitter finish is an awesome way to hide the error effortlessly. It is really easy to do on your own and some celebrities have even rocked it at big events. Modern Family’s Sarah Hyland added glitter to her grey mani at the Emmys, creating such a cute look. Glitter accents also pair beautifully with [pearly tip details](#2) for extra shimmer.

## 6. Caviar Nails

![Caviar Nails](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/28123205_0878_600x415.jpg)

With a fancy name like that, it was bound to be a huge trend! Originally created by [Ciaté London](https://www.ciatelondon.com), their creative director aimed for something feminine, dimensional, and delicate. You need a bit of patience to master this design on your own, but the end result is totally worth it. Stars like Jessica Biel and P!nk have worn this tactile 3D style on major red carpets.

## 7. Ombre Nails

![Ombre Nails](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/28123519_8114_600x400.jpg)

If you don’t want to commit to ombre hair, try it out on your nails! The look can be done with any color and makes a fun fashion statement. Painting nails in pastel ombre brings back summer vibes, while warm reds and oranges are perfect for autumn and winter. Lauren Conrad and Vanessa Hudgens have both worn this creative nail polish design!

To keep your gradient smooth and long-lasting, consider these quick tips:

- Use a damp makeup sponge for seamless color blending

- Apply liquid latex around cuticles for easy cleanup

- Finish with a thick top coat to smooth out sponge textures

## 8. Trendy Talons

![Trendy Talons](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/28123553_8466_600x398.jpg)

Stiletto and talon nails remain a iconic statement style. Celebrities like Demi Lovato, Lady Gaga, and Rihanna have all stepped out with these claw nails, inspiring fans to try the look. You can create intricate designs on them or stick to a bold solid color. For red carpet events, talon nails look sassy and fierce, though shorter rounded variations might be more convenient for everyday schedules.

## 9. Studded Jewels

![Studded Jewels](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/11/28123704_3853_600x429.jpg)

Incorporating studs into your nails is a great way to add edge to any color. Stars like Kesha, who is always trying out new trends, have rocked this look multiple times. You can stud the entire nail or line them up vertically. Placing a single stud at the bottom center of each nail gives a cool, edgy vibe regardless of your polish shade.

The list of creative celebrity nail polish designs goes on and on. Expressing yourself through all these awesome colors and designs is so easy and fun! Will any of these creative designs be your next manicure choice?
