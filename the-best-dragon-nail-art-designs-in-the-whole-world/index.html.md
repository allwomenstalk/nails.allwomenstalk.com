# 30 Best Dragon Nail Art Designs & Inspo for Every Style

Dragons are so fierce, but so cool at the same time. In fact, they are one of the most popular choices when it comes to getting a tattoo. Not ready for that commitment? How about dragon nail art instead? You can indulge your love of this mythical creature without having to sign up for a lifetime of having him live on your body. Whether you're looking for subtle dragon nail inspo or bold statement claws for the Year of the Dragon, here are 30 of the best dragon nail designs to elevate your manicure. You are going to love them!

| Dragon Style | Best Finish | Skill Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waterspotted Scales | Glossy Topcoat | Intermediate |
| Metallic Egg Texture | Matte / Shimmer | Beginner |
| Detailed Character Art | High-Shine Gel | Advanced |

## 1. Green Waterspotted Dragon Skin

_ Source: Spektor's Nails: Waterspotted Nails_

Looks like dragon skin, doesn't it? This technique is so easy to do and really fun too! By dropping nail polish into water and spraying a mist of alcohol or hairspray, you get a textured reptilian effect that looks authentic.

Key tools to try this dragon nail art technique at home:

- Deep emerald green base polish
- Alcohol spray bottle for water-spotting
- High-gloss topcoat
