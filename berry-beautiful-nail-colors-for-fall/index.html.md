# 21 Pretty Berry Nail Colors for Fall: The Ultimate Autumn Polish Guide

The hottest beauty color this fall is berry, for your hair, lips, and especially your nails. Rich berry fall nails offer a sophisticated transition from bright summer shades to cozy autumn aesthetics, combining deep plum, vibrant raspberry, and moody burgundy tones. Whether you are looking for long-lasting gel nail colors for fall or classic creamy lacquers, these pretty nail colors will elevate your autumn manicure. Here are 21 hot hues to help you try this berry beautiful trend.

Key reasons why autumn nail colors in berry shades are essential this season:

- Complements every skin tone from fair to deep melanin
- Pairs effortlessly with cozy fall knits, trench coats, and leather jackets
- Offers versatile finishes ranging from high-shine gel to shimmering glitter accents
